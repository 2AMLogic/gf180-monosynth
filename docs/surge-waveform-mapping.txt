Why this file exists: model/reference_rigs.py asked Surge for a saw, received a
50 % pulse, and published it as a saw for a whole study. The request and the
label agreed with each other and nothing compared either with the signal. The
sweep is the derivation of the corrected mapping; the controls are the
demonstration that the check which now guards it rejects a wrong setup.
