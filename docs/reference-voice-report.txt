  NOTE ON THE ~52 % SQUARE (miniv3/square, h2 at -24.0 dB where ours is absent):
  the Model D service manual records that R137 was HAND-SELECTED PER UNIT to trim
  the square to 50 %. A reference showing 52 % is therefore modelling A UNIT OUT OF
  TRIM, not the design intent, and our exact 50 % is arguably the more faithful
  value. Carried as an open disagreement, not as our defect.

  GENERALLY: where a reference emulation models UNIT VARIATION, a difference from it
  is not automatically a defect in ours. That applies to the oscillator-drift targets
  too, and probably to more of the measured spread than has been assumed.
